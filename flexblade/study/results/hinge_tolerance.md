# Hinge-radius build tolerance, 10 in v8 lead (hinge_radius 21.5 mm)
**Definition.** `flexure.hinge_radius` (schema.py:232, fact.py hinge_axis) is the radial position of the strip crossing point H0 on the skewed hinge axis. Physically it is: hub jaw exit radius (hub_radius 12 mm, through-slot in the hub), root jaw exit (blind slot in the blade fitting, free span 25.12 mm), strip crossing angle 105 deg, cross gap 2.7 mm and skew 62 deg. The crossing sits ~0.38 of the span from the hub exit, so r_h = 0.62 r_hub_exit + 0.38 r_root_exit (+ a z-gap term via the skew).
**Stack (mm, effect on r_h).** Hub exit XY +-0.15 gives 0.093. Root exit XY +-0.15 gives 0.057. Slot side clearance 0.1 per side (fittings.SIDE) times 1/tan 52.5 deg gives 0.08. Layer/z error +-0.1 (0.12-0.2 layers) times cot 62 deg gives 0.05. Cross-gap shim +-0.1 gives ~0.05. Shim cut length +-0.1 gives ~0 (the free span is set by the jaw exits and the blind end only fixes land depth), 0.05 if the strip is not seated against the blind end.
**Result.** RSS +-0.16 mm = +-0.75 %. Linear worst case +-0.33 mm = +-1.5 %. Both are well inside the +-5 % (+-1.07 mm) CQ corner. A mis-seated strip or a hub/blade pair printed on different days is the realistic way to reach 1.5 %.
**Sensitivity** (study/cf_invariant_mode.run_point, T1.0, three single evals, trim re-solved): r_h 21.18 / 21.50 / 21.82 mm gives mech mode/rev 0.9423 / 0.9485 / 0.9545. dp/dr_h = +0.0095 per mm. dln p/dln r = 0.43. So +-0.75 % gives +-0.0030 (+-0.3 % in p). +-1.5 % gives +-0.0061 (+-0.65 %). The CQ +-5 % would give about +-2.1 % in p in isolation. The 2.7-4.7 % in damped_resonance.md also includes the other corner parameters.
**Margin.** Nominal |1-p| = 0.052 here (T1.0, this J), so build spread of hinge radius alone eats 6-12 % of the margin and the mode stays below 1P in all three runs. It is not the dominant term once CAL is done: residual after CAL is what counts. The mode moves toward 1P as r_h grows.
**Pinning features (proposed).** (1) Ream or print the hub and root slots undersize, and datum both strips on a 3 mm steel dowel pressed across the hub slot exit, so the exit radius is the dowel, not the printed edge. (2) A 0.3 mm stainless crossing clip (or a 0.05 mm dot of epoxy with a printed-gauge) holds the crossing point at r_h. (3) Add printed slot depth stops at the blind root end and a printed seating gauge. (4) Mate hub and blade as a printed pair, or measure r_h with calipers on a gauge pin and trim with a 0.1 mm shim under the root fitting. (5) Tune afterwards: pluck-test the pitch-sym line, then shift with a shim (+0.1 mm = +0.0009 in p/rev).
