# Hakea single rotor, existing 2307 1300KV, 6S, 5-6" prop: authority budget and levers
Design `designs/6in_tt_strap_steelx_2307.json`, one eval per point (T0.7/1.0/1.25 x hover thrust, J_hub 1.57e-6, Goodman).
Script `study/lever_2307.py`, raw `results/lever_2307.json`. The sa.py 0.0123 is this min (0.0136) at J 0.58e-6 (0.0124).

## 1. Budget: cr_pl = (dQ_1P,avail / Q_hover) x (M / dQ_1P) x (Q_hover / (W R))
| point | dQ/Q (limit) | M/dQ mech gain | Q/(WR) | cr_pl | cyclic pitch at pulse limit |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 6" T1.0 | 2.19 (current) | 0.090 | 0.156 | 0.031 | 1.2 deg |
| 6" T1.25 (robust min) | 0.85 (thermal) | 0.103 | 0.155 | 0.0136 | - |
| 10" v8 T1.0 | 0.94 (strip fatigue, s 0.59) | 0.854 | 0.150 | 0.121 | 4.9 deg |
| 10" v8 T1.25 | 0.82 (fatigue) | 0.727 | 0.150 | 0.089 | - |
**The gap is the mechanism gain, not the motor.** M/dQ is 9.5x lower at 6". The 6" has 2.3x MORE pulse headroom than v8, and Q/(WR) (~CQ/CT) is the same.
So the LOG.md claim "the first factor dominates" is wrong at T1.0. The motor only adds to the gap at T1.25, where the thermal cap reaches 0.85.
M/dQ = (M/theta) x (theta/dQ). Normalised, M/theta is the same at both sizes: cr ~0.025 per deg of cyclic pitch.
In absolute terms M/theta is 3.4x lower at 6" (rho c Omega^2 R^4). Pitch per N m is 2.8x lower (5.9 vs 16.3 deg/N m), mostly from the skew coupling: tan(skew) 0.81 here vs 1.88 on v8.
A small rotor at high rpm gives little aero moment per N m of hub torque. Only a flexure with a larger skew/coupling (DE d2307_de3) can recover part of that.

## 3. Required authority (nothing in repo; estimate, 6": R 76 mm, Omega R 120 m/s)
- CG offset e needs cr = e/R: 5 mm -> 0.066, 1 mm -> 0.013. Tolerable offset = cr_pl x R: 0.9 mm (robust), 2.4 mm (hover), 3.9 mm (combo, hover).
- A 2 m/s gust (or flying at 2 m/s) gives mu = 0.017. Blade-element hub moment, with and without inflow feedback: cr ~ 1.0-1.6 mu = 0.017-0.027.
- 5 mm + 2 m/s needs ~0.08-0.09: 6" margin x0.15 (robust) / x0.35 (hover). Check: on 10" v8 the same spec needs 0.058-0.070 vs 0.0715 delivered.
- 1 mm (trimmed CG) + 2 m/s needs 0.030-0.040: base x0.3-0.4 robust, ~x0.8-1.0 hover. Combo x0.85-1.1 robust, x1.3-1.7 hover.

## Verdict: MARGINAL. Not feasible at the assumed 0.79 kg / 5 mm CG / outdoor gusts.
Calm-air, indoor demonstrator only: AUW <= 0.7 kg, CG trimmed to about 1 mm (hang test), heavier hub, ESC >= 50 A, throttle <= 1.25x.
The physical limit is the small-rotor mechanism gain. Motor current/thermal only cuts the climb point. 5", 3 blades, more pitch and more chord all make it worse.
**Fred measures:** (1) AUW with battery; (2) ESC/battery current the motor can pull in a pulse (ESC current limit and sag); (3) motor winding temperature rise at hover current (Rth; 1.5 K/W guess). Also check that he can hang-balance the CG to about 1 mm.

## 5. Stock-width realisation (12.7 mm)
Feeler stock 12.7 mm wide, 0.0508/0.038 mm; n chosen for n*w*t^3 ~ 8x4.5x0.05^3 (n=3 +11 %). Presets `resolve_candidate(d,6)`; sa.py same T1.25 command, SA_JHUB 0.34/0.91/2.0e-6. Original reproduced: cr_pl 0.167, only harmonic_jitter 0.73.
- Nominal checks (L 10.05): all stock variants fail creep_strain 1.44, aeroelastic 1.07-2.06, root_envelope 3.25 (soft; orig 2.3-2.7), fitting_clearance 0.09-0.10 ok. n=2 also static_stress/interlayer/jitter; t 0.038 n=6/7: strip_ltb 0.96/0.85, pitch_cf_ratio erratic.
- sa.py n=4 x 0.0508 (`designs/5in_v8s_2307_m245_stock.json`): cr_pl 0.084, INFEASIBLE: creep_strain 0.48, split 8.8, **geom:flexure_inside_hub 0.43**, aeroelastic 0.12. Jitter is fixed (pitch sym 1.42/rev, cqc ph 1.04, gain 0.16, mech 0.107) but the 12.7 mm span (w^3 stiffer in-plane/torsion) does not fit the hub at r 10.75, 105 deg.
- n=3 and L +-15 % not sa.py-scored (~20 min/run at load 60; geometry/creep failures are width-driven so expected the same). Not recommended without a hub redesign (wider hub or smaller cross angle).
- Build note: glue lands ONLY with thick epoxy (no thin CA, it wicks along the 0.05 mm gaps); keep free span dry or PTFE dry-film. CalculiX shows a bonded stack is ~n^2 stiffer in bending, so fewer laminae (3 x 12.7 vs 8) reduce that risk.

## 7. Lead verification
`5in_v8s_2307_m245_301` (301 FH, 9 x 4.0 x 0.05 mm). T0.7/1.0/1.25, J_hub 0.34/0.91/2.0e-6, Goodman, 1 worker per run.
- tol.py (nominal + 12 random builds, seed 0, `tol.py` with THRUST_FACTORS and the J set patched; presets fixed), margin score: default +-5 % 13/13 feasible (no hard violations), cr_pl 0.131-0.175 (nominal 0.169), res margin 0.158-0.321. Worst build t0.950 w0.999 l1.037 h0.974: cr_pl 0.131, res 0.158 (pitch sym 1.15/rev vs 1P; need 0.15), so the binding gate is the 1P resonance margin (thin strip, long, low hinge radius).
- CQ_TOL=datumed: 13/13 feasible, cr_pl 0.140-0.170, res margin 0.175-0.308. Worst build t0.950 l1.015: cr_pl 0.140, res 0.175 (same resonance gate). Build tolerance is not a problem; the thin-side thickness drives the worst case.
- CalculiX stiffness (`ccx.compare_strip_flexure`, one-lamina ccx x 9 vs the beam): ratios x9 k_hinge 0.94, k_cross 1.04, k_flap 1.04, kx 1.04, ky 1.00, kz 1.01 (raw 0.105-0.116), all within 6 % (20 % criterion), axis error 0.001 deg.
- CalculiX stress, `DN=5in_v8s_2307_m245_301 TF=0.7,1.0,1.25 CCXMODES=loads ccx_preset_strip.py preset` (ccx + the beam's in-plane end loads, hub / blade peak, ccx/beam): T0.7 1.23 / 1.51, T1.0 0.89 / 1.15, T1.25 0.75 / 1.01; preset only 1.20 / 1.21. The hub is conservative (to -25 %) and the blade end up to +15 % (+51 % at T0.7, where the beam stress is a low 86 MPa). The old mixed-BC run (ratios 0.27-0.53) is a BC artefact (missing in-plane moment), superseded.
- Vehicle mass (margin score nominal, J set, T0.7-1.25): 0.20 kg cr_pl 0.208, no hard violation (res 0.283); 0.29 kg cr_pl 0.130, no hard violation (res 0.182, pitch sym 1.18/rev). 0.245 kg: 0.169.

## 8. ±10 % tolerance
`CQ_TOL_MAG=0.10 TOL_TF=0.7,1.0,1.25 TOL_J=0.34e-6,0.91e-6,2.0e-6 TOL_WORKERS=2 tol.py 5in_v8s_2307_m245_301.json 12 0` (new env overrides, defaults unchanged; thickness/width/length/hinge radius all U(0.90,1.10), presets fixed, default mode, margin score).
- 9/13 feasible (nominal + 8 of 12 builds); 4 builds infeasible. cr_pl 0.106-0.186 (nominal 0.169), res margin 0.099-0.320.
- Worst build t0.900 w0.999 l1.074 h0.949: cr_pl 0.106, res 0.099, hard violation resonance 0.34 (pitch sym 1.09/rev vs 1P, need 0.15). Second resonance fail t0.962 l1.080 (res 0.140, viol 0.068).
- Resonance fails (2): thin strip plus long free length (t<=0.96 with l>=1.07), thickness and length together.
- Aeroelastic fails (2, viol 0.006 and 0.033): thick, wide, big-hinge builds (t1.08 w1.09-1.10 h1.07-1.08), antisym+hub mode 0.88/rev; driven by thickness+width, i.e. stiffness high side.
- Slot angle was not toleranced (presets fixed). The design is not robust at ±10 %; needs datumed thickness/length (see section 7) or about ±6 %.
