Design `designs/6in_tt_strap_steelx_2307.json`, one eval per point (T0.7/1.0/1.25 x hover thrust, J_hub 1.57e-6, Goodman).
Script `study/lever_2307.py`, raw `results/lever_2307.json`. The sa.py 0.0123 is this min (0.0136) at J 0.58e-6 (0.0124).

## 1. Budget: cr_pl = (dQ_1P,avail / Q_hover) x (M / dQ_1P) x (Q_hover / (W R))
| point | dQ/Q (limit) | M/dQ mech gain | Q/(WR) | cr_pl | cyclic pitch at pulse limit |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 6" T1.0 | 2.19 (current) | 0.090 | 0.156 | 0.031 | 1.2 deg |
| 6" T1.25 (robust min) | 0.85 (thermal) | 0.103 | 0.155 | 0.0136 | - |
| 10" v8 T1.0 | 0.94 (strip fatigue, s 0.59) | 0.854 | 0.150 | 0.121 | 4.9 deg |
| 10" v8 T1.25 | 0.82 (fatigue) | 0.727 | 0.150 | 0.089 | - |
**The gap is the mechanism gain, not the motor.** M/dQ is 9.5x lower at 6". The 6" has 2.3x MORE pulse headroom than v8, and Q/(WR) (~CQ/CT) is the same.
So the LOG.md claim "the first factor dominates" is wrong at T1.0. The motor only adds to the gap at T1.25, where the thermal cap reaches 0.85.
M/dQ = (M/theta) x (theta/dQ). Normalised, M/theta is the same at both sizes: cr ~0.025 per deg of cyclic pitch.
In absolute terms M/theta is 3.4x lower at 6" (rho c Omega^2 R^4). Pitch per N m is 2.8x lower (5.9 vs 16.3 deg/N m), mostly from the skew coupling: tan(skew) 0.81 here vs 1.88 on v8.
A small rotor at high rpm gives little aero moment per N m of hub torque. Only a flexure with a larger skew/coupling (DE d2307_de3) can recover part of that.

## 3. Required authority (nothing in repo; estimate, 6": R 76 mm, Omega R 120 m/s)
- CG offset e needs cr = e/R: 5 mm -> 0.066, 1 mm -> 0.013. Tolerable offset = cr_pl x R: 0.9 mm (robust), 2.4 mm (hover), 3.9 mm (combo, hover).
- A 2 m/s gust (or flying at 2 m/s) gives mu = 0.017. Blade-element hub moment, with and without inflow feedback: cr ~ 1.0-1.6 mu = 0.017-0.027.
- 5 mm + 2 m/s needs ~0.08-0.09: 6" margin x0.15 (robust) / x0.35 (hover). Check: on 10" v8 the same spec needs 0.058-0.070 vs 0.0715 delivered.
- 1 mm (trimmed CG) + 2 m/s needs 0.030-0.040: base x0.3-0.4 robust, ~x0.8-1.0 hover. Combo x0.85-1.1 robust, x1.3-1.7 hover.

## Verdict: MARGINAL. Not feasible at the assumed 0.79 kg / 5 mm CG / outdoor gusts.
Calm-air, indoor demonstrator only: AUW <= 0.7 kg, CG trimmed to about 1 mm (hang test), heavier hub, ESC >= 50 A, throttle <= 1.25x.
The physical limit is the small-rotor mechanism gain. Motor current/thermal only cuts the climb point. 5", 3 blades, more pitch and more chord all make it worse.
